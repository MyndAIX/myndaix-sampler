Select: drag on the wave. It snaps to the nearest beat; hold Shift for whole bars, Alt for no snap. Beats are quarters of the detected bars, so on a drifting song they're close, not exact.
Sections: the band over the wave names each part (intro, verse, chorus, outro). They're the chopper's guesses from which stems play; a ? means a guess, and the same letter means the same part repeating.
Hear it: Space plays the selection, L loops it, Esc stops. Click a shaded region to select what the AI chopped.
Keep it: drag the orange chip onto a pad, or Shift+click a pad. Click a pad to hear it, × to empty it. A replaced pick is kept on disk, never deleted. Nothing is stretched.
