MYNDAIX-SAMPLER

Space stops all loops

Left pads: 1 2 3 4 / Q W E R / A S D F / Z X C V  ·  banks Shift+1–4 (or fn+F1–F4)  ·  pitch G up, B down, T reset  ·  5 sync
Right pads: 7 8 9 0 / U I O P / J K L ; / M , . /  ·  banks Shift+7–0 (or fn+F5–F8)  ·  pitch H up, N down, Y reset  ·  6 sync
Pad 1 is bottom-left on each side. On a Mac keyboard hold fn with the F-keys (unless your F-keys are set to standard). Pitch moves speed and pitch together, one semitone a step, like a classic sampler. In the loops bank a pad starts or stops its loop on the next bar.
SYNC on a deck makes it follow the other deck: same tempo (a slow song locks at half time), its loops land on the other deck's bar lines, and it keeps following when the other deck's pitch moves. Like a record, the key moves with the speed. Press SYNC again, or move that deck's pitch, to let go. ×2 / ÷2 fix a tempo that was read at double or half speed.
